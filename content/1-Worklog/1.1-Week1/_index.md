# Week 1 - Program Orientation, Architecture, and Backend Foundation

### Week 1 Objectives:

- Understand the internship requirements and FCAJ project report rules.
- Analyze the internship application management problem and identify core user groups.
- Initialize the backend foundation, database schema, authentication flow, and role-based access.

| 2 | Analyze user problems and identify the Candidate and HR/Company groups. | 08/06/2026 | 14/06/2026 | [AWS Well-Architected Framework](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/welcome.html); [FCAJ Project Requirements](https://cloudjourney.awsstudygroup.com/8-fcjworkforce/) |

### Week 1 Achievements:

- The project scope and main business flows were identified.
- Candidate and HR/Company roles were clarified.
- React/Vite, FastAPI, Node.js/Socket.IO, and AI service were selected for the system.
- PostgreSQL, DynamoDB, Redis, and S3 were mapped to different data needs.
- The backend foundation, authentication flow, and initial migration were prepared.
