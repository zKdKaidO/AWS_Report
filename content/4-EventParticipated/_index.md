| No. | Event | Time | Participation Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FCAJ Community Day – June 2026: Data Driven, AI Risen | June 27, 2026 | Online |

The three events provided me with a more comprehensive understanding of the process of developing Cloud and AI products, from problem identification and architecture design to Agentic AI development, deployment, monitoring, and system security.

The knowledge gained from these events will help me improve my internship project in a more structured and secure manner, particularly in areas related to AWS architecture, AI Agents, monitoring, data management, and system security.
